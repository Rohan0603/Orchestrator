# Commercial product handoff

## Boundary

This repository is a research MVP. A future commercial project must not
inherit prototype assumptions without requalification.

## Retain

Retain SQLite-authoritative lifecycle state, explicit policy and exact
approvals, versioned adapter capabilities, durable dispatch intent, evidence
provenance, fail-closed acceptance, and uncertain recovery states.

## Replace or redesign

Replace fixture-only execution with a qualified adapter. Design supported
Windows process ownership before live execution. Add independent reviewer
execution, artifact retention, observability, migration/backup procedures,
clean installation, signing, update/rollback, and support diagnostics.

## Required commercial gates

Re-run threat modeling, dependency/licensing review, clean-machine install,
provider/route qualification, cancellation and recovery fault exercises,
backup/restore validation, reviewer gold-set evaluation, repair economics, and
privacy/credential handling review. Do not claim launch readiness until each
applicable gate has reproducible evidence tied to an exact version.

## Reproducibility

Use `experiments/README.md`, retain raw trial records, pin configuration and
verifier versions, and report blocked prerequisites instead of substituting
synthetic success.
